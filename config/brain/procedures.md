# Office procedures

## Coffee and kitchen supplies
- One 1 kg bag of coffee serves about 40 cups. Plan 2 cups per person for a meeting, and keep at least one full bag spare.
- If coffee stock is below 30% and a meeting with 4 or more people is planned today, order 2 bags without waiting to be asked.
- Paper cups: reorder when fewer than one pack is left.

## Broken equipment
- Report it to the responsible team with a ticket first. Facilities answers within 2 hours on workdays.
- Do not replace equipment while a repair ticket for it is still open: wait for Facilities' assessment, unless they say it cannot be repaired.
- If Facilities has not answered within 2 hours, post a reminder on the dashboard. After a day, tell the office manager.

## Meetings with guests
- Kitchen above 25 °C or above 60% humidity before a meeting: ask someone to open a window at least 30 minutes before it starts.
- Make sure there is coffee and cups for every attendee.

## End of day
- Summarise what was handled, simulated spend, decisions that needed a human, and what is still open.
